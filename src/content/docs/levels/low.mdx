At Crawl, agents are assistants. A developer plans with Copilot, codes with Copilot and accepts each edit. CI tests and scans every change, and a person approves every pull request and every release.

This level is about building trust and the foundations everything else depends on: shared gates, written-down context and a habit of reviewing what agents produce.

## The line at this level

Who does what at each station, what the HQ puts in and gets back, and what the foundations supply and keep.

<LevelStages level="low" />

## Multiplayer mode

**Singleplayer.** Agents run locally, in the IDE, the Copilot CLI and the Copilot app. The developer at the keyboard is the only one who sees the session. Hand-offs happen through pull requests, as they always have.
