At medium autonomy, agents do the work and people approve it. Agents draft specs from issues and signals, Copilot cloud agent builds tasks in parallel, and review agents test and fix. People set priorities, steer sessions and approve the merge.

This level is about scale with control: more work in flight, approvals based on risk, and every agent with its own identity and budget.

## The line at this level

Who does what at each station, what the HQ puts in and gets back, and what the foundations supply and keep.

<LevelStages level="medium" />

## How the team works

Agents run in cloud sandboxes with Copilot cloud agent. Sessions are shared: anyone on the team can watch, steer or continue them in mission control, the Copilot app or GitHub Mobile. Work starts from issues, events, schedules and production signals. [How collaboration changes across the levels →](/framework/multiplayer/)
