## Building the factory is a lit job

The engineering loop is part of the HQ: it never goes dark. A platform engineering team, or a partner, treats the factory as a product. Its customers are the people and agents on the line.

## Three moves

**Tune trust and control.** SLOs, approvals and policies. Decide who or what can approve each kind of change, under which identity, within which budget. At Crawl this means rulesets and CODEOWNERS. At Run it means policy as code and approval by exception.

**Expand automation.** Workflows, triggers and environments. Give agents somewhere safe to work and something to start them. At Crawl this is CI/CD and golden dev environments. At Run it's fleets per work type, end-to-end test environments and progressive delivery.

**Strengthen foundations.** Skills, tools and context. Every incident, review and eval is a chance to make the next session better. At Crawl this means starter instructions and approved MCP servers. At Run, agents curate skills and memory, and people review every update.

## One loop, every level

The loop doesn't change between levels. What changes is the size of each move. Use the tabs above to see the engineering work for each level, or open the [playbook](/playbooks/) for step-by-step instructions.
