## The HQ: people bring clarity

The HQ is where people work: intent, creativity, steering and judgment. It holds the dev tools where people meet their agents, the places ideas and designs start, collaboration and approvals, planning and tracking, the **controls** that set metrics, SLOs, policies and budgets, and the **observability** that shows what the floor is doing.

It stays bright at every automation level.

## The floor: agents do the heavy lifting

The floor is where agents work. **Models** and **agents** do the thinking and the typing. **Delivery environments** give them safe sandboxes to build and test in, and **production environments** are where the software runs. **Automations** start work from events, schedules and signals. **Security** applies the same gates to every change, human or agent.

This is the layer that dims as automation goes up.

## The foundations: stronger every loop

The foundations are what the factory is built on, and what it keeps from every loop: **code artifacts** with provenance, **tools** agents can call through MCP and APIs, **context** such as skills, memory, ontologies and goals, and **identity and access** for people and agents alike.

The better the foundations, the further the floor can dim.

## How the layers connect

- **Down from the HQ:** intent, steering and approvals.
- **Up from the floor:** evidence, signals and cost.
- **Into the foundations:** specs, skills, learnings and attestations, versioned in Git.
- **Around all of it:** the [engineering loop](/framework/engineering/) that builds and tunes the factory.
