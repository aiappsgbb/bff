## How the score works

Each answer scores 0, 1 or 2. Up to 5 points is **Crawl**, 6 to 11 is **Walk** and 12 or more is **Run**. Two rules come first:

- **No shared gates, no automation.** If people and agents don't go through the same gates, the work type stays at Crawl, whatever the score.
- **No evidence, no lights-off.** Run needs an evidence bundle on every change. Without it, the work type stays at Walk.

<Aside type="tip">
Your answers and level stay in this browser. Level pages, playbooks and diagrams across the site follow the level you get here.
</Aside>
