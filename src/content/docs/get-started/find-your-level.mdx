## How the score works

Each answer scores 0, 1 or 2, and the points behind each option show once every question is answered. Up to 6 points is **low autonomy**, 7 to 12 is **medium autonomy** and 13 or more is **high autonomy**. Two rules come first:

- **No shared gates, no automation.** If people and agents don't go through the same gates, the work type stays at low autonomy, whatever the score.
- **No evidence, no lights-off.** High autonomy needs an evidence bundle on every change. Without it, the work type stays at medium autonomy.

<Aside type="tip">
Your answers and level stay in this browser. Your level is marked on [autonomy levels](/levels/), and the home page dimmer and the level tabs on [singleplayer to multiplayer](/framework/multiplayer/) and [engineering the factory](/framework/engineering/) open at it.
</Aside>
